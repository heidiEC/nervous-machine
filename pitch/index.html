On the Shoulders of Machines

Big models propose. The edge earns certainty. People move the frontier.

Open with the tagline. Everything in the deck is one loop: big models propose, the edge earns certainty against measurement, and people move the frontier where certainty was never earned.

The problem

AI proposes faster than anyone can check it against the world.

01

The data lands too late

Measurements arrive late, partial or never. Links carry kilobytes, not the full record.

02

Fit isn’t measurement

Models fit their training data, not this site, this sensor or this season.

03

No one knows where knowledge ends

Models can’t say what they’ve never seen, and can’t grade their own answers.

04 · and so

People fall behind, and the frontier stops moving

What the models can’t settle needs a person’s judgment. Teams decide on what was confirmed last quarter.

If people can’t reach the frontier, the frontier doesn’t move.

5

It is one directed loop, not four components. The first three steps make the ledger trustworthy; DEBRIEF only avoids hallucination because it briefs from that ledger, never from a model’s report of itself (the 72-cycle run is the proof). The return arrow is the step nothing automates: a person’s abduction closes a frontier gap and becomes the next prior.
