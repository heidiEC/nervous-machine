Nervous Machine

Edge-Native Dual-Use Runtime Assured Autonomy (RTAA) & Causal Safety Kernels

Company Overview

Nervous Machine provides a deterministic, first-principles mathematical protocol designed to map the boundaries of system knowledge and enforce a verifiable safety envelope at the edge. Operating entirely on-device with zero GPU, cloud, or neural-network dependencies, our kilobyte-scale kernel acts as a real-time runtime verification layer for complex robotics, unmanned aerial systems (UAS), and distributed satellite constellations.

Core Capabilities

  • Runtime Assured Autonomy (RTAA) via Causal Envelopes: Act as a deterministic RTAA safety filter for un-vetted control and reinforcement learning (RL) policies. Instead of using brittle numerical thresholds that cause false flags, the kernel monitors the structural validity of physical relationships in real-time, mathematically isolating operational drift or malicious signal manipulation before it propagates to actuation.
  • Hierarchical Prior Inheritance & Constellation Mesh Seeding: Engineered a modular transfer architecture for robots, drones, and satellite constellations. When a new policy or orbital asset is deployed, it inherits a structured causal prior (~50kb). To guarantee safety, inherited edges seed at low operational certainty (0.30–0.40), forcing the system to rapidly re-earn certainty against local physics before executing high-risk maneuvers. Critical rules-based edges seed at high certainty (0.90) to minimize learning-rate drift while keeping the safety envelope secure.
  • Ultra-Low SWaP Execution (Kilobyte-Scale): The protocol is compiled entirely to Rust in the kilobyte range. Causal vectors are compressed down to ~1kb per edge, allowing real-time telemetry mesh synchronization across highly constrained downlinks, distributed robotic swarms, and embedded ARM targets (Raspberry Pi/Jetson-class) without clogging bandwidth.
  • Introspective Re-Hypothesis & Gap Registration: When encountering low-certainty anomalies, the kernel executes autonomous re-hypothesis loops against external domain knowledge to patch the causal graph locally. Persistent, unresolvable anomalies are cleanly logged to a definitive Gap Register, defining the exact mathematical boundaries of what the system does not know to inform human operators or future sensor placements.
